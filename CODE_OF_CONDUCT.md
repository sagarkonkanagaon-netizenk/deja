# Code of conduct

deja follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people using deja. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/sagarkonkanagaon-netizenk). All reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
